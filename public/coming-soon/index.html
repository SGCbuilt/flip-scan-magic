Capabilities

A self-learning investment engine, not another spreadsheet

FlipScan Pro finds distressed properties before the courthouse crowd, analyzes them like a contractor, and follows up with sellers automatically.

Auction Radar

Foreclosure and tax-sale auctions pulled straight from real county platforms across VA, NC, TN & FL — the sources casual investors never find. Follow any property and get emailed the moment a sale date is set.

Self-learning research engine

Scans your markets every morning, ranks what it finds by equity, urgency and market speed, and drops the winners into your pipeline — with a daily digest in your inbox. Deals found while you sleep.

Deep Scan investor memos

One tap turns any address into a full memo: photos, sale & tax history, value through the years, permits, violations and distress signals — plus an intelligent verdict on flip, wholesale or rental.

Outreach that writes itself

A 60-day follow-up cadence starts the moment a lead is found. The engine drafts each email around that seller's real situation — auction date, tax delinquency, equity — and sends it for you.

Drive for Dollars, upgraded

Pin-drop GPS, curb photos straight from the car, instant skip-trace and comps at the curb. Save a house in seconds and let the analysis run before you're home.

Contractor-grade numbers

Rehab estimates calibrated to real construction costs, 70%-rule checks, and an A–D deal grade — so your max offer is built on what the work actually costs, not a guess.

Distress signals, stacked

Eleven government data feeds — code violations, delinquent taxes, permits, evictions — cross-matched so triple-signal leads rise to the top instead of hiding in spreadsheets.

Pipeline to closing

Kanban CRM from first signal to closed deal, construction timeline with carry-cost tracking, and actual-vs-estimate P&L that makes your next estimate smarter.

Wholesale, one packet

Match each deal to your buyer list, generate a branded deal PDF, and blast it to the right buyers in one click — comps, math and a clear ask in a single file.

Workflow

Three steps. One clear path.

From investor criteria to a single Showing Packet — without the blast list.

  1. 01

    Profile investors

    Capture buy boxes, markets, and motion (wholesale, flip, or both) so every match starts from real criteria.

  2. 02

    Match the deal

    Run the deal against profiles. See who fits — and why — before you pick up the phone or hit send.

  3. 03

    Send one packet

    Ship a single Showing Packet: numbers, context, clear ask. Right deal. Right buyer. One file.
